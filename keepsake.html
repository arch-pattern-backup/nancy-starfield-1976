A 50TH BIRTHDAY MEDITATION & KEEPSAKE

Sitting in
the Quiet

FOR NANCY'S 50TH BIRTHDAY
⚬
Fifty years of living, fifty years of light,
Yet we don’t need to paint a forced smile tonight.
Let the cake sit quietly upon the shelf,
There is no demand to trick or shield yourself.
If the air tonight is heavy with the hours we have known,
We don’t need to force applause or stand alone.
No unlit candle needs to feign a burning spark—
We will simply sit together in the quiet of the dark.
Between the acoustic strings and the falling of the year,
We hold space for what is real, for what is present here.
Unadorned and unvarnished, open-handed and free,
Sitting in the quiet is where I want to be.
