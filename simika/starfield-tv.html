STARFIELD
2001

In Princeton's light, a star was born,
September two-thousand-and-one, a golden morn.
A life began to span the earth,
From first breath to journeys worth.

From East Windsor's yard to Brampton's snow,
Then Kitchener's streets, where you would grow.
Two countries shaped the heart you'd keep,
A bridge between worlds, steady and deep.

Then westward called — San Jose's sun,
Bellevue's green, where new chapters run.
Issaquah's hills, then Kent's own light,
Each city a home, each move a new sight.

Through classrooms, then to children you would serve,
Behavioral health work, with heart and steady nerve.
From Kent's early learning to Renton's call,
You caught the smallest hands before they could fall.

Forty families, nine sites, a hundred more,
You made the hard work look like an open door.
A quarter century, the road still wide,
With love as compass, and stars as guide.

JOURNEY
From a first cry in Princeton to Pacific rain,
Each city you've lived in has shaped you again.
More yourself, more true, with every mile,
More quietly certain, more openly your smile.
Not the halfway mark, not the resting place,
Just the opening of a longer, braver grace.
The future unwritten, the sky still wide,
Twenty-five stars, and a world inside.

So here's to the years, and here's to the light,
To the woman you are, and the road in sight.
No need to have it all figured out today,
The story keeps growing, come what may.

For home was never just a place,
It's love and time and warm embrace.
The family bonds that hold so tight,
The memories that guide your light.

Each star above, a day you've known,
Nine thousand, one hundred, thirty-one.
Twenty-five years of love and light,
Every single one, yours to brighten.
